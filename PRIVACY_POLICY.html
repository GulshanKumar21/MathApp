Privacy Policy for PrepVanta

PrepVanta: Fast Calculation & Exam Prep (Package: com.mathspeed.app.math_speed_practice)
Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Version 1.0

Official Live Web Policy: https://sites.google.com/view/prepvanta/home

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile software application PrepVanta ("Application", "we", "us", or "our"), created by Gulshan Kumar for Android devices. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our users, students, and competitive exam aspirants.

Core Privacy Guarantee: PrepVanta is an educational app designed to enhance mental arithmetic speed. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

The Application collects only the minimal information necessary to deliver and sync educational calculation practice, diagnostic analytics, and in-app upgrades:

  • Account Information: When you register an account or log in via Firebase Authentication, we may collect your name, email address, and authentication user ID. This is solely used to preserve your learning progress across sessions and devices.
  • Calculation Performance & Learning Telemetry: To provide intelligent spaced repetition and weakness diagnostics, the Application records practice session results, accuracy percentages, response times in seconds, and questions answered incorrectly (saved in your personal Revision Notebook).
  • In-App Purchase Data: In-app purchases for the PrepVanta PRO Lifetime license are handled entirely and securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We never collect, see, or store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, CVV codes, or net banking credentials. We only receive a transaction token and order ID from Google Play to unlock PRO features.

2. Android System Permissions & Purpose

PrepVanta requests the following Android device permissions strictly to deliver its advertised learning features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to connect securely to Firebase Cloud Firestore for synchronizing your study progress, leaderboards, and PDF cheat sheets. Offline practice is fully supported when no network is available.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to deliver interactive spaced-repetition calculation challenges and mistake recall reminders with quick answer buttons directly to your notification shade.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to schedule scientific spaced repetition reminders (1 hour and 3-hour recall intervals) configured by the student.
  • VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during rapid speed calculations and alerts you when timed test drills begin or end.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Ensures that study reminders and alarm schedules set by the student are restored automatically if the device restarts.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Enables official 1-tap Google Play In-App Billing for upgrading to the optional PrepVanta PRO lifetime license.

3. Third-Party Services Used

The Application utilizes trusted Google services that adhere to stringent privacy and security standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android operating system services and API delivery.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes in-app purchases in accordance with Google Play's Developer Policy.
  • Google Firebase (Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore): Cloud authentication and encrypted database synchronization. Privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard administrative and technical security measures to protect your learning data:

  • All network communications between the app and cloud backend are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Purchase records and database access are validated using cryptographic verification (HMAC-SHA256).
  • Local data is stored securely in encrypted application sandboxes on your device.

5. User Rights: Account & Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play User Data policies, users have complete control over their stored data:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, authentication details, and all mistake logs at any time directly inside the app by navigating to Profile ➔ Delete Account & Personal Data.
  • Deletion by Email Request: You may also request complete data erasure by emailing us at gulshankumar.dev01@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated records will be permanently purged from our servers within 7 business days.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PrepVanta is an educational app aimed at general audiences preparing for competitive exams (secondary school students, college students, and adult competitive aspirants). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements in our app or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the Application signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
